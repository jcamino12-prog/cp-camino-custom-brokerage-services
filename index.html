ABOUT US

CP Camino Customs Brokerage Services is a reliable and efficient provider of end-to-end customs solutions for businesses engaged in international and domestic trade. Strategically located in Bacoor, Cavite, we specialize in facilitating the smooth movement of goods across borders by ensuring full compliance with customs regulations, accurate documentation, and timely delivery. Our professional team is equipped with deep expertise in freight forwarding, import/export requirements, and logistics coordination, making us a trusted partner in your supply chain journey.

We are committed to delivering personalized, transparent, and responsive services to meet the growing demands of clients across various industries. Whether it's by air, sea, or land, CPCamino bridges the gap between local and global markets with integrity and efficiency.

SERVICES OFFERED

Customs Brokerage Services

We process import and export declarations, ensuring full compliance with customs rules and regulations. Our team works closely with clients to guarantee smooth cargo release and documentation.

Customs and Tariff Consultancy

Our consultancy services help clients understand tariff classifications, duties, taxes, and trade agreements. We provide expert advice to optimize costs and ensure legal compliance.

Air, Sea, and Land Domestics / International Freight Forwarding

We offer integrated logistics solutions for air, sea, and land cargo. Our global network enables us to move shipments reliably and economically across borders.

Door-to-Door Deliveries

Seamless door-to-door cargo transport services from origin to destination, including all handling, customs, and delivery requirements.

Handling Special Cargoes

Expertise in handling sensitive, oversized, or regulated cargo with special care and adherence to safety and legal standards.

Permits and Licenses

Assistance in securing necessary permits, licenses, and accreditations required for import/export operations.

Lifting and Amendments

Fast and accurate processing of amendments, lifting of alerts, or other customs documentation changes.

Seizure and Forfeiture Assistance

Guidance and representation in cases of customs seizures or forfeiture proceedings, helping clients resolve issues legally and efficiently.
